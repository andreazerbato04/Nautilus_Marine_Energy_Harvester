<ul><li><b><h3>Self-powered wireless sensor system for water monitoring based on low-frequency electromagnetic-pendulum energy harvester:</h3> </b>
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0360544222007861?via%3Dihub<br><br>
The most complete open-source reference for a low-frequency wave energy harvesting sensor is the Water-Wave Self-Powered Wireless Sensor design by Li et al. (2022), which integrates a 1.5 Hz eccentric electromagnetic pendulum harvester, a boost-converter PMS with hysteresis control (operating down to 0.8V), and a low-power ZigBee MCU node (measuring TDS and temperature) into a compact, sealed floating hull. The project provides detailed validation on low-voltage energy accumulation, low-impedance coil optimization (4.22 Ω), and continuous battery-free operation under small wave amplitudes (>1.2 cm).</li>
