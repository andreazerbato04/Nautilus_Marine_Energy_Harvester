# REFERENCES #
<ul><li><b><h3>Self-powered wireless sensor system for water monitoring based on low-frequency electromagnetic-pendulum energy harvester:</h3> </b>
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0360544222007861?via%3Dihub<br><br>
The most complete open-source reference for a low-frequency wave energy harvesting sensor is the Water-Wave Self-Powered Wireless Sensor design by Li et al. (2022), which integrates a 1.5 Hz eccentric electromagnetic pendulum harvester, a boost-converter PMS with hysteresis control (operating down to 0.8V), and a low-power ZigBee MCU node (measuring TDS and temperature) into a compact, sealed floating hull. The project provides detailed validation on low-voltage energy accumulation, low-impedance coil optimization (4.22 Ω), and continuous battery-free operation under small wave amplitudes (>1.2 cm).</li> <br><br>
<li><b>  A fully packed spheroidal hybrid generator for water wave energy harvesting and self-powered position tracking:<br> </b>
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2211285519311565?via%3Dihub <br><br>
A prominent reference for a multi-source wave energy harvesting smart buoy is the Spheroidal Hybrid Generator (SB-HG) by Chandrasekhar et al. (2019), which combines a Triboelectric Nanogenerator (TENG) and an Electromagnetic Generator (EMG) driven by a sliding inner mechanism, supplemented by a top-mounted solar cell for low-wave conditions. The system powers a long-range (LoRa) position-tracking node and achieves peak outputs of 100V / 2µA (TENG) and 20V / 15mA (EMG) to continuously charge capacitors and a lithium-ion battery. The project provides key design principles for packing low-frequency kinetic harvesters inside a fully sealed, waterproof spheroidal floating hull for self-powered marine tracking and environmental monitoring.</li>
