# Location: the Venice lagoon #
Venice laggon is the biggest lagoon in the Mediteranean sea, is almost 550 Km^2. The laggon is created by the interaction of fresh water from different river like Brenta, Piave,Sile and the saline water of the Adriatic sea. <br><br>
