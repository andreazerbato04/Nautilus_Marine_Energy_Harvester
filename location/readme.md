# Location: the Venice lagoon #
Venice lagoon is the biggest lagoon in the Mediteranean sea, covering almost 550 Km². The laggon is created by the interaction of fresh water from different rivers like Brenta, Piave, Sile and the saline water of the Adriatic sea.<br> Since the 1987 the Venice lagoon and the city became a UNESCO heritage site. This status has introduced national and international regulations, wich make continuous enviromental monitoring essential for the maintenance of the city.<br><br>
